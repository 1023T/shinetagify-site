What we need to quote a label

Send these five and the price plus a sample plan come back the same day.

1 · Artwork

AI or PDF with vector text. Bleed and die line if you have them.

2 · Size & shape

Width × height, corner radius, or a die shape. New die is normal work.

3 · Stock & adhesive

Where it goes: glass, painted metal, plastic, carton. Or tell us the use.

4 · Roll or sheet

Roll direction, core size and pieces per roll if you apply on a line.

5 · Variable data

Numbers, barcodes or QR — send the list and the starting number.

6 · Quantity

Per design, per size. Short and long runs both run in house.
