Sizes & how they hang

Self-locking tags come with the wire shown; the tab width and drop are what buyers usually confirm first.

  • Tag body 6 × 10 cm2.36 × 3.93 in — the size most dealers order for key boards
  • Total drop 13.5 cm5.1 in including the strap, measured from the top of the tag
  • Strap width 0.5 cm0.19 in — fits standard key rings and hooks
  • Writable fieldsYEAR / MAKE / MODEL / BODY / COLOR / MILEAGE printed to your wording

Need a different size? The die is made to your drawing — send the dimensions and quantity.

Where they end up

On key boards, workshop hooks and machine valves — the tag has to survive a workshop, not a showroom.

How an order runs

Four steps, no trading company in between.

1 · You send artwork + quantity

Logo file (AI, PDF or high-res PNG), the wording for the writable fields, and how many pieces per colour.

2 · We quote and send a sample

Price per piece and a sample plan the same day. A physical sample goes out before the run — 7 days typical.

3 · Approve, print, ship

You approve the sample, we print and die-cut in house, then ship with the packing you asked for.

Custom shapes and new dies are normal work for us — send the shape and we quote the tooling with the print.
